Privacy Policy

This application is a personal-use OAuth client used with rclone to access Google Drive.

Google User Data

The application requests access to Google Drive data so that rclone can list, read, create, modify, and delete files as directed by the user.

Data Storage

Google Drive file data is accessed directly by rclone. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally by rclone on systems controlled by the user. This website does not collect or store Google Drive data or OAuth tokens.

Data Sharing

Google user data accessed through this OAuth client is not sold or shared with third parties by the operator of this application.

Purpose

This OAuth client is maintained solely for personal use with rclone and is not offered as a service to the public.
